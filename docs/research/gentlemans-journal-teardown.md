# Gentleman's Journal (thegentlemansjournal.com): Reverse-Engineering Report

**Snapshot date:** 27 October 2026
**Method:** Public rendered pages fetched as text/markdown (homepage, `/latest/`, one article page) plus search-engine snippets of `/club/`, localized homepages and older articles.
**Confidence key:** 🟢 observed directly · 🟡 inferred from strong evidence · 🔴 not observable with this method (see §15 for how to capture it)

> **Limits of this report.** I could read page *content, structure, metadata, URLs and image URLs*. I could **not** read CSS, JavaScript bundles, fonts, network calls, response headers, `robots.txt` or `sitemap.xml`. Every visual-design statement below (type, colour, spacing) is therefore either inferred or flagged 🔴. Section 15 contains a console script that extracts the missing values in about a minute.

## 1. Executive summary

Gentleman's Journal (GJ) is a UK luxury men's lifestyle magazine brand (est. 2012; "© 2012 – 2026 By G Studio Limited"). The website is a **headless, server-rendered Next.js app on top of Sanity CMS**, built by the agency **Byass Group** (byass.group). Its business model has three legs:

1. **Editorial + sponsored/brand content** (style, grooming, watches, cars, food & drink, travel, business interviews, property, field sports)
2. **A paid membership called the "Clubhouse"** (print magazine twice a year, paywalled web content, a physical membership card, partner discounts, events, a free gift with sign-up)
3. **A shop** (claims 1000+ products, 15% member discount) plus events ("GJ Live"), podcasts and video

The site is also available in **at least five other languages** (fr, de, es, it, pt) via path prefixes.

### 5.2 Latest (`/latest/`)

- H1 "Latest", subhead (paraphrase: "style, culture and the good life"), H2 "Latest stories", label "Page 1".
- **18 cards per page**, same card component as home (image alt + category + title + dek + date).

The server queries "items older than `lastDate`/`lastId`" (a stable keyset pagination; avoids offset drift) and `page` is only a display counter. `Previous` is shown disabled on page 1.

Template details:
- **Breadcrumb is an ordered list** (`<ol>`) → probably also exposed as `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD (the French page snippet shows `{"name":…,"item":…}` entries).
- **Interview format**: bold questions prefixed with interviewer initials; answers prefixed with interviewee initials. This is a repeatable CMS pattern ("Q&A" block type).
- **Pull-quote** is a `<blockquote>` placed after the relevant paragraph, with the same sentence duplicated in the body (editor-selected, not auto-generated).
- **Images**: inline images are portrait-format originals (≈ 5213×6525) rendered at 648 px width with `fit=max` (no cropping, preserve aspect ratio) while the hero uses `fit=crop` + focal point.
- **Further reading** shows exactly 3 items, with a date format of `D MMMM YYYY`, and the card text format "{image alt} {Category} - {date} {title}".
- **Paywall signs** (from indexed snippets of other articles) 🟡: gated pieces show a "Discover the perks of being a member" table (*Two Magazines per year · Full access to paywall content · Membership Card · Members invites to exclusive events*) and a "For more information please email subscriptions@thegentlemansjournal.com" line. Content is gated server-side (or the text is truncated for crawlers). Free vs paid is a per-article flag.
